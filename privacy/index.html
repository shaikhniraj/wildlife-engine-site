Wildlife Engine privacy policy

This policy covers the Wildlife Engine apps for Android, Windows, Mac and NAS servers. Wildlife Engine is made by Niraj Shaikh, Nashik, Maharashtra, India. Contact: wildlifeengine21@gmail.com.

In short: your photos are analysed on your own device. We don't run servers that receive your photos, we have no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Photos leave your device only when you choose to: species identification with your own AI key, sharing a photo to another app, or a feedback file you send us yourself.

What the app reads on your device

  • Your photos, with the photo permission you grant, to rate them, group bursts, sort them into categories and name species.
  • Photo details such as the date taken and camera model, to group bursts and tell camera photos from phone photos.
  • Faces in photos, only to group photos of the same person on your device when you use People. Face data never leaves your device.

The results (ratings, categories, species names, people you named) are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.

What can leave your device, and only when you choose

  • Species identification with AI (optional). If you add your own Google Gemini API key and ask for species names, each chosen photo is cropped to the animal and sent to Google with the region you typed in Settings and the date the photo was taken. Google processes it under your agreement with Google (Gemini API terms). We never see these photos or your key; the key is stored encrypted on your device. On computers you can also choose another AI provider or one running on your own network.
  • Sharing a photo with another app (Open with, Edit in, Share), which then handles it under its own policy.
  • Help improve (optional, off by default). If you turn it on and tap Share feedback, the app makes a small file that you send us yourself, by email or another app. It contains the species names you or the AI gave, each as 512 numbers that describe the animal (the photo can't be rebuilt from them), the measurements of photos you moved between Selects and Rejects (sharpness, eye, exposure, stars), your country, the app version and a random identifier. It never contains photos, file names, places, dates, or photos of people. We use it only to improve species naming and photo rating.

What we never do

  • We don't collect your photos, location or contacts.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics tools, and we don't sell or rent any data.
  • We don't need an account. Licence keys are checked on your device, without the internet.

Internet use

The app connects to the internet only for the AI species identification you request, and on computers to download place names (from GeoNames) and model updates. These downloads send no information about your photos.

Children

Wildlife Engine is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and requests

You can turn off species identification and Help improve at any time in Settings. If you have sent us a feedback file and want it deleted, or have any question about privacy, email wildlifeengine21@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Delete your data

Everything Wildlife Engine makes (ratings, categories, species names, people you named) stays on your device. To delete it, clear the app's data in your phone's Settings, use Settings > Clear all analysis in the app, or uninstall the app.

We hold no account or photos of yours. If you have emailed us a feedback file, email wildlifeengine21@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and we will delete it and confirm within 30 days. Photos you sent for AI identification were sent to Google under your own Gemini API key; manage them through your Google account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Changes that affect what leaves your device will also be shown in the app.
